HOW TO USE AXIOSTAT MIL88

Tear open Axiostat pack and Apply Axiostat with pressure until dressing sticks to the wound

Once applied, do not lift or remove Axiostat, not even to check haemostasis. Apply field dressing to secure Axiostat in position

To remove Axiostat, irrigate with saline/water and gently peel it off

To treat gunshot wounds, axiostat may be used as shown

FAQs

Axiostat MIL88 is a hemostatic patch designed to quickly control bleeding from localized or focal wounds, such as punctures, cuts, and stab injuries.

Axiostat MIL88 is ideal for small, targeted wounds.

Axiostat MIL88 uses chitosan-based bioadhesive technology to adhere directly to the wound surface and promote rapid clot formation.

Axiostat MIL88 should be placed directly on the wound, followed by firm pressure and secure fixation. The patch should not be lifted once applied.
