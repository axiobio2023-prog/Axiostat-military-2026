Why Axiostat ?

Our haemostat dressings are designed for first responders in conflict areas where medical readiness and extreme usability are paramount. Axiostat is a bioactive haemostatic dressing that works on a unique charge-based mechanism to stop the bleeding quickly.

When Being Quick Can Save Lives

Axiostat made of 100% chitosan with PBT® technology controls bleeding more faster and effectively than dressings coated with haemostatic agents.

A New Perspective On Combat Casualty Care

Axiostat plays an important role in the combat field as it stops bleeding on contact, irrespective of a person's coagulation status.

A Life-saving Device With Zero Adverse Effect

Axiostat haemostat dressings are biocompatible and prevent undesirable side effects like thrombosis, inflammation, or embolism.

Adding Care Through Packaging: Lightweight, Camouflaged, and Rugged

Compactly designed to cope with battlefield emergencies, to fit inside any first aid kit or BSNF kit and camouflages perfectly when carried in bulletproof jackets.

Laboratory Tested & Combat Proven

In a laboratory test using the standardised combat injury model, Axiostat demonstrated a 100% survival rate. We also completed several field trials in which soldiers treated with Axiostat had a high rate of survival.

When Blood Matters, Time Matters- Prolong The Golden Hour For Critically Injured

The golden hour is the critical period immediately after a serious injury. Prompt treatment to stop bleeding during this emergency hour can save lives in the combat field.

Axio is focused on stopping preventable deaths during the "Golden Hour" through its innovative bleeding control technology- Axiostat.
