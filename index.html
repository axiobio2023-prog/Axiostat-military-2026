HOW TO USE AXIOSTAT MIL300

Remove excess blood, locate the source of bleeding, pack the wound with Axiostat Z-Fold Gauze to stop the blood flow, by applying constant pressure.

Apply continuous pressure at least for 3 minutes until the bleeding stops. Use a secondary dressing to secure the gauze in a single place and to prevent further contamination of the wound.

Unwrap the secondary dressing, irrigate Axiostat Z-Fold Gauze with plenty of salines and gently remove the gauze from the wound.

FAQs

Axiostat MIL300 is a military-grade hemostatic dressing product designed to rapidly control moderate to severe bleeding in combat, tactical, and emergency trauma situations. It is considered one of the best combat gauzes for deep and irregular wounds.

Axiostat MIL300 is intended for heavy bleeding, including deep penetrating wounds, junctional injuries, and irregular wounds where tourniquets may not be effective.

Axiostat MIL300 uses 100% chitosan with Protonated Bioadhesive Technology (PBT®), which actively adheres to blood and tissue to accelerate clot formation.

Axiostat MIL300 is applied by firmly packing the gauze into the wound, applying sustained pressure for several minutes, and securing it with a secondary dressing once bleeding is controlled.
