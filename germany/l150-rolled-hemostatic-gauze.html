FAQs

Axiostat L150 is a rolled hemostatic gauze designed to control bleeding by wrapping around wounds, especially in extremity and surface injuries.

Axiostat L150 is effective for cuts, lacerations, gunshot wounds, and traumatic injuries where wrapping and sustained compression are required.

Axiostat L150 is optimized for wrapping wounds, while Axiostat MIL300 features a Z-fold design better suited for deep wound packing.

Axiostat L150 is applied by wrapping it tightly around the bleeding site while maintaining firm pressure until hemostasis is achieved.
