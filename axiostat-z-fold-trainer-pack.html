Axiostat Z-Fold Trainer Pack is a soft, white z-folded gauze designed exclusively for training purposes. It replicates the look and feel of the original Axiostat Z-Fold dressing, giving users realistic, hands-on experience in simulated bleeding control scenarios. Axiostat is not indicated for medical use; this trainer pack is intended for demonstration and practice only.

Axiostat Z-Fold Trainer Pack Features:
  • Designed to mimic the handling and placement technique of the actual Axiostat Z-Fold dressing
  • Allows trainees to practice packing wounds in controlled training environments
  • Can be used with artificial blood for enhanced realism
  • Compatible with training mannequins and simulation kits
  • For Training Use Only
